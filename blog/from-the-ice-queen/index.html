From the Ice Queen

“Control yourself”, said JS
“Stop pretending I said YES”

“I said NO and you agreed
And I’m sorry if you bleed”

“I am sorry for your pain
But please try to use your brain”

“I can’t feel the same as you
Just because your love is true”

“So stop messing with my life
‘Cause I’m not your fucking wife”

“If you really are my friend
Then please try to understand”

2012

Published in Just Friends in 2013
