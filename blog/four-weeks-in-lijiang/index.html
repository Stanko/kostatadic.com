Four Weeks in Lijiang

Just four weeks... But they seem like a year... I met so many nice and interesting people... I made a dozen of really good friends. And I lost many of them... I fell in love. And I got my heart broken... It started like a dream. And it's ending with insomnia... I came with a big smile. And I am leaving in tears... 24 hours ago I was the happiest man in the world. And now I am the saddest one... Thank you all for your hospitality and kindness... I will never forget you.

28/07/2018
