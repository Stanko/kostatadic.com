Akire

Although the future seems so scary now
Keep in mind that you have been here before
Instead of repeating WHEN and WHERE and HOW
Relax and just open the nearest door
Enter the New World you are looking for

25/01/2018

Just be YOU, and you will rule this fucking world. :)
