Up & Down

This is a "quotation" from Love in a Puff, one of her favourite movies. The main character sends a text message to a girl. It doesn't make any sense until she turns her phone upside down. And this was my last attempt to win her kindness back. This is the place where I usually smoke, and she is passing along this street every day. But it hasn't melted her coldness, not even for a bit. :(
