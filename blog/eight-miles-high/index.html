Eight Miles High

Just the way I miss you walking in the street
Eight miles in the air I still hope we'll meet
Nothing really matters, nothing seems complete
If it isn't you having the next seat
Traveling alone, I dream we're together
Airlining the world for a sunny weather

11/01/2013
