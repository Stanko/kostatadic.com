Put the Kettle On

Come on lazy Sun
Show your face and rise
And make pretty Jenny
Open her blue eyes

Come on sleepy Jenny
Wake up, it is dawn
Get up on your feet
Put the kettle on

Come on sleeping beauty
The day has been born
Go to the kitchen
Put the kettle on

Come on JDS
The night is long gone
It’s time for the action
Put the kettle on

Come on sleepy owl
Come on pretty swan
It is time for flying
Put the kettle on

Come on Joan’s Sister
Don’t you dare to yawn
It is time for music
Put the kettle on

Come on, come on
Put the kettle on

And on and on…

The day has been born!

2012

Published in Just Friends in 2013
