Sirikanya

She is so sweet, she is so nice
I could spend years watching her eyes
Reading the stories hidden inside
I'd spend my life right by her side
Kissing her softly and holding her tight
All of the day and all of the night
Nothing could stop us from feeling alright
Yelling and singing and having a laugh
And stars would shine on our behalf

30/03/2016
