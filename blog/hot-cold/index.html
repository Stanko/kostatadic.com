Hot and Cold

FIRST VOICE: Good evening, dear listeners. Tomorrow it will be cloudy and very warm...

SECOND VOICE (calls out from OFF): Cold!

FIRST VOICE: Cloudy and warm...

SECOND VOICE: Warmer!

FIRST VOICE: Cold...

SECOND VOICE: Hot! Hot!

FIRST VOICE: Very cold...

SECOND VOICE: Bravo!

FIRST VOICE: Cloudy and very cold.

2009

Originally published in BCS (Bosnian-Croatian-Serbian) in Par grama drama (A Few Grams of Drama) in 2010. Translated by Timothy John Byford. Translation copyright by Kosta Tadic.
