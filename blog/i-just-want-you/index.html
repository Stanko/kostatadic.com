I Just Want You

I don't want to fuck any goddamn noddy

Just a plastic doll and a brainless body
Uniting is bad without the true feeling
Sex without Love ain't really appealing
To the skin and flesh when they're also dealing

With the same attraction as the complete being
All I really want is your loving presence
Nothing in this world could give me more pleasance
Than being inside your feminine warmness

Your hug makes me feel I'm no longer formless
Only beside you I feel I exist
U're the only one I cannot resist

2013

Published in The Man in 2013
