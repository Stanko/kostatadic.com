I'll Be Good to You

I’ll be good to you, I’ll prove that I care
I will make you smile, every day, I swear
When you need someone I’ll always be there
Looking at your eyes and beautiful hair

I will make you warm when you’re feeling cold
I will stay by you while you’re growing old
I will still be there, all wrinkled and bald
I’ll make you believe everything I’ve told

I will always be right there by your side
By your loving body and beautiful mind
I will give you shelter when you need to hide
And I hope some day you will be my bride

I hope me and you will grow old together
Sharing both the rain and beautiful weather
I hope we will love each other forever
Smiling and crying and fighting together

28/03/2016
