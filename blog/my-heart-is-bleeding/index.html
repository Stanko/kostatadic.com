My Heart Is Bleeding

My heart is bleeding, but it’s okay
‘Cause wounds are clean, and they form “J”
‘Cause wounds were touched by your hand
‘Cause I’m in love with the great friend

My heart is bleeding, but it’s not broken
It hurts, but it’s whole, and it’s awoken
It’s easy to bleed when wounds are glowing
It’s great to love someone worth loving

I hope my wounds will never heal
I hope I’ll always feel what I feel

2012

Published in Just Friends in 2013
