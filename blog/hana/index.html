Hana (هناء)

Hope one sunny day you'll come really close
And open your bud right under my nose
Now I can smell a beautiful rose
And I want to taste the earth where it grows

Although I know you don't feel the same
Nevertheless, the pain disappears
As I think of true meaning of your name
