I Hate People

I hate people and despise

Human habit of disguise
All their words are full of lies
They all always hide their eyes
Except you – you are true

Please allow me to love You
Every human is the same
Only different by their name
People always play their game
Lie and whore and have no shame
Except you – You are true

2013

Published in The Man in 2013
