I Want You JDS

I don't want your pity, I can live alone

Waking on my own at dawn after dawn
All I want is just a drop of your warmth
Now when I'm back in the freezing North
Thank you for your wishes and thinking of me

Your honesty, friendship and letting me be
Open 'bout my feelings that you cannot share
Understanding, kindness and for being there

Just please don't ask me to kill what I feel
Don't be cold to me just to help me heal
Something that's not ill but real and sincere

2013
