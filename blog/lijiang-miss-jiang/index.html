Lijiang & Miss Jiang

Finally, a sunny day…
But my Sun is faraway…

She has left, she has gone…
I am so, so alone…

I have money, but I’m poor…
Because she’s in Singapore…

I’m a man, but I cry…
‘Cause she hasn’t said goodbye…

I am healthy, but in pain…
‘Cause my love has been in vain…

I do like Lijiang…
But I miss Miss Jiang…

23/07/2018
