There were many girls waiting for the test  
(And they all were nervous, pretending they’re not)  
But the Eastern girl eclipsed all the rest  
Beautiful and calm, elegant and hot  

And I still can see her standing next to me  
Moving very softly and stretching her arms  
And I have no doubt I will always be  
Deep under the spell of her unique charms  

_**2015**_

I have never seen her again. "Always" lasted for a month.
