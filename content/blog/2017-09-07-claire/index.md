**C**hina is your home, and I know you are  
**L**eaving very soon for where you belong  
**A**lthough I know you'll be very far  
**I**'ll never forget beautiful Miss Hong  
**R**emember our dreams, and forget the rest  
**E**ven though we failed, we had tried our best

_19/08/2017_
