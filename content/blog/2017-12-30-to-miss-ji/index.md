Some people are bad But please don't be sad Elephants are strong Stronger than King Kong They'll survive and then Punish the bad men They'll step on their toes Hit them with their nose They will make them cry And just say "Goodbye" And run to be free Just like you and me KT and Miss Ji

_**13/12/2017**_

Ji (冀) – pronounced “Jee”
