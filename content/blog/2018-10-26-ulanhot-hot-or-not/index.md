**PART 1**

Ulanhot,
it ain’t hot,  
at all…  

Ulanhot  
is so cold,  
and small…  

Ulanhot  
is an old  
shithole…  

Ulanhot,  
it ain’t hot,  
at all…  

I am shaking  
and I’m waiting  
for her call…  

Ulanhot,  
it ain’t hot,  
at all…  

**PART 2**

Ulanhot,  
it’s so hot,  
with Her…  

She’s so soft,  
like a coat  
made of fur…  

Ulanhot,  
it’s so hot,  
with Her…  

**_October 2018_**
