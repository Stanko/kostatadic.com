“I'm your friend”, said JS “Nothing more. And nothing less”

“Nothing more” – such a stress Turned my feelings into mess

“Nothing more” broke my heart All my dreams just fell apart

“Nothing more” made me cry Made me wish I wanna die

“Nothing more” gave me pain Made me feel I'd lived in vain

“Nothing more” made me sad Made me wish I'd been dead

I felt bad, I went mad Put the gun against my head

To my finger I said “Yes” Then I thought of “nothing less”

“Nothing less” eased my vigor Took my finger off the trigger

“Nothing less” wiped my tears Made me braver, killed my fears

“Nothing less” said by J Made me put the gun away

_**2012**_

Published in _Just Friends_ in 2013
