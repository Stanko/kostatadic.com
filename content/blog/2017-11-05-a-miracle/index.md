_The Middle Ages. The interior of a church in which the body of a deceased P. is lying in state. (Orthodox Christians can read the “p” as “Patriarch”, and Catholics as “Pope”). The faithful approach humbly, one by one, and kiss his hand._

_Smooch... Smooch... Smooch..._

P. _(sits up suddenly and shakes his hand in disgust):_ Incredible! The plague is raging through Europe, and you’ve all come here to dribble over me.

_The faithful jump back in fear, but the P. lies down once again and closes his eyes._

_Pause._

THE FAITHFUL: A miracle! A miracle! A miracle!

_Pushing one another, all the faithful together rush towards the catafalque._

_SMOOCH! SMOOCH! SMOOCH!_
