You are not aware how pretty you are Pretty in a rare, mysterious way Pretty like the light of a shooting star Pretty like the night in the month of May

You are not aware how bright you are Gorgeous like the sky in the break of day You shine like the Sun even though you're far There's no other girl like beautiful May
