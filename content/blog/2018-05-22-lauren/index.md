**L**ooking at your face and beautiful eyes  
**A**nd talking to you made me realize  
**U**'re an honest girl, wearing no disguise  
**R**elaxing in silence (I apologize)  
**E**ven though I know we didn't say much  
**N**ow I can't stop dreaming of your touch

_15/11/2017_
