**I** hate people and despise

**H**uman habit of disguise  
**A**ll their words are full of lies  
**T**hey all always hide their eyes  
**E**xcept you – you are true

**P**lease allow me to love You  
**E**very human is the same  
**O**nly different by their name  
**P**eople always play their game  
**L**ie and whore and have no shame  
**E**xcept you – You are true

_2013_
