**J**ust the way I miss you walking in the street  
**E**ight miles in the air I still hope we'll meet  
**N**othing really matters, nothing seems complete  
**I**f it isn't you having the next seat  
**T**raveling alone, I dream we're together  
**A**irlining the world for a sunny weather

_11/01/2013_
