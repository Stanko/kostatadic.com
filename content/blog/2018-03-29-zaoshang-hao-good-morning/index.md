Zaoshang hao  
Zao Zao Zao  

Hey hey hey  
Have a nice day  

Laozi ai ni  
Please think of me  

I’ll think of You  
My dear Pu  

_**18/03/2018**_

Zaoshang hao (早上好) – good morning Zao (早) – morning! Laozi (Sichuanese dialect) ai ni (爱你) – I love you
