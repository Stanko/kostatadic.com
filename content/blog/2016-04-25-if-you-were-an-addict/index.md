If you were an addict burning of desire  
I would kiss your feet till I ease that fire

[![kostatadic-ifyouwereanaddict-01](images/kostatadic-ifyouwereanaddict-01.jpg)](images/kostatadic-ifyouwereanaddict-01.jpg)

If you were a naked junkie in the street  
I would spread my body underneath your feet

[![kostatadic-ifyouwereanaddict-02](images/kostatadic-ifyouwereanaddict-02.jpg)](images/kostatadic-ifyouwereanaddict-02.jpg) 

Like a magic carpet I would take you high  
And ask: "My Lady, where'd you want to fly?"
