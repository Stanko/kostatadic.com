**J**enita I miss you like you miss the Sun  
**E**very night I dream we are having fun  
**N**eath the big old tree on the Brunswick Street  
**I** am not afraid and you look so sweet  
**T**hen I am awake and I know you're gone  
**A**nd it makes me wish I'd never been born

**J**enita I miss you asleep and awoke  
**E**verywhere I go I wish we could talk  
**N**othing seems complete if you aren't there  
**I** wish I could see your eyes and your hair  
**T**oday I was smiling in front of the Shrine  
**A**cting like a tourist, pretending I'm fine

**J**oking with my new and beautiful friend  
**E**njoying his presence, the touch of his hand  
**N**evertheless, it was just a mask  
**I** was just a soldier fulfilling my task  
**T**rying to live on and to fill my day  
**A**lthough I knew you're so faraway

_10/01/2013_

Published in _Just Friends_ in 2013
