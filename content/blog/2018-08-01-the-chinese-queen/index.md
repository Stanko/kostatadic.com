When I see  
Li Xinli  

Looking like a  
Chinese Queen  

I have never  
Never seen  

Such a pretty  
Pretty sceen  

I have never  
Never heard  

Someone singing  
Like a bird  

Singing from her  
Very Heart  

She’s talented  
She is smart  

When she sings  
I just freeze  

She’s a storm  
And she’s a breeze  

Makes me smile  
Makes me cry  

Takes me down  
Takes me high  

Wo ai (我爱)  
When she sings  

Wo ai (我爱)  
Many things  

‘Bout Xinli  
The Chinese Queen  

‘Bout Xinli  
The Chinese Queen
