Made very fast, ain't a real art but it was made right from the heart

Enjoy your day, take all you can Care like a woman and hit like a man Don't let nobody stay in your way Say what you sing and sing what you say Do what you play and do it your way And the worst day will have to obey

This ain't no art, ain't a reply just an excuse to say Hi Hi and Hello, Good Luck and Bye this morning Hi needs no reply

Good thoughts attached the signature touched

Thinking of you very much
