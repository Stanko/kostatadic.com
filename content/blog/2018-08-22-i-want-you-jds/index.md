**I** don't want your pity, I can live alone

**W**aking on my own at dawn after dawn  
**A**ll I want is just a drop of your warmth  
**N**ow when I'm back in the freezing North  
**T**hank you for your wishes and thinking of me

**Y**our honesty, friendship and letting me be  
**O**pen 'bout my feelings that you cannot share  
**U**nderstanding, kindness and for being there

**J**ust please don't ask me to kill what I feel  
**D**on't be cold to me just to help me heal  
**S**omething that's not ill but real and sincere
