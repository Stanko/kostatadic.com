**P**lease forgive my constant running after you  
**O**ffering my feelings and wanting the same  
**N**o one else can make me feel the way you do  
**D**eep inside my Heart there's only one name

_05/02/16_

**P**lease give me a chance and you'll see my love  
**O**pen up your Heart and let me inside  
**N**ote that you're the One I am thinking of  
**D**ay and night I miss being by your side

_15/02/16_

**P**lease forgive me Pond, please don't set me free  
**O**ffer me a chance to make it alright  
**N**othing in this world matters much to me  
**D**eserted by you I can't see the Light

_22/02/16_

**P**lease don't hate me Pond, I was just a fool  
**O**nly when I lost you I knew what I'd had  
**N**ow I am crying, please don't be so cruel  
**D**o believe I'm sorry and so so so sad

_01/03/16_

**P**lease don't let me leave without goodbyes  
**O**h I love you so, and I really care  
**N**ight and day I've been thinking of your Eyes  
**D**reaming of your long and beautiful Hair

_07/03/16_

**P**aris on my mind, hour after hour  
**O**nly because Pond is under its skies  
**N**otre-Dame, the Seine and the Eiffel Tower  
**D**on't shine half as bright as her loving Eyes

_25/06/16_
