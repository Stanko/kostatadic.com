– A man and a woman have different knives. They can't avoid hurting each other. When the wounds become dangerous, then it is all over – she said.

I can't remember the exact words, but it was something like this. Maybe she was just quoting some old Chinese saying, but I still think it was beautifully said. It was beautiful and sad at the same time.

And also true. But I still think they can avoid hurting each other. If they just try a little bit harder.
