I believe  
that Khun Steve  
should live.  

And not choose  
a bad-booze  
death-leading abuse.  

Ain’t a lie:  
if you die,  
K will cry.  

**_2017_**

**Khun** (**คุณ**, pronounced "koon") – Mr.
