I would live with any illness  
Just to win your sweet forgiveness  

I would hit the strongest man  
Just to be your friend again  

I would eat your spicy food :)  
Just to make you think I’m good  

I would stand the greatest pain  
Just to make you think I’m sane  

I would crawl and I would fly  
Just to get your one reply  

I would fly across the skies  
Just to look into your eyes  

I would fight two dozen boys  
Just to hear your charming voice  

I would swim through ocean waves  
Just to see your lovely face  

I would walk through desert sand  
Just to touch your gentle hand  

There’s no thing I wouldn’t do  
Just to talk again to You
