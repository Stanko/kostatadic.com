[taxonomies]

I said: “I only want To gently hold your hand” She smiled and told me: “Don’t You’re just a funny man”

Why does she call me funny? Why does she call me so? Why does she call me, I wanna know Why does she call me so?

I said: “This world is wild We have to hold each other” She said: “You’re just a child And I am not your mother”

Why does she call me funny? Why does she call me child? Why does she call me, I wanna die Why does she call me child?

I said: “I will be true And always be around” She laughed and asked me: “You? You’re just a funny clown”

Why does she call me funny? Why does she call me clown? Why does she call me, I’m going down Why does she call me clown?

_**2006**_

Published in _The Man_ in 2013
