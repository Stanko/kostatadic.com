**S**he is so sweet, she is so nice  
**I** could spend years watching her eyes  
**R**eading the stories hidden inside  
**I**'d spend my life right by her side  
**K**issing her softly and holding her tight  
**A**ll of the day and all of the night  
**N**othing could stop us from feeling alright  
**Y**elling and singing and having a laugh  
**A**nd stars would shine on our behalf
