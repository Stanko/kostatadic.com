You are NOT a whore, to me you’re the Queen  
The prettiest woman I have ever seen  
The friendliest girl I have ever met  
Proud like a lion, and sweet like a cat  

You are NOT a whore, you’re the Only One  
Your eyes are much more brighter than the Sun  
You’re the greatest singer I have ever heard  
Scary like a tiger, and cute like a bird  

You are NOT a whore, you’re more than a dream  
Sexy, independent, elegant, supreme  
You’re my loving Princess, the One I adore  
And ’cause of my words I feel like a whore
