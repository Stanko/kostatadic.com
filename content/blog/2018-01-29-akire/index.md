**A**lthough the future seems so scary now  
**K**eep in mind that you have been here before  
**I**nstead of repeating WHEN and WHERE and HOW  
**R**elax and just open the nearest door  
**E**nter the New World you are looking for

_25/01/2018_

Just be YOU, and you will rule this fucking world. :)
