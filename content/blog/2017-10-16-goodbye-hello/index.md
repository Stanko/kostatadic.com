Goodbye pretty baby, it’s been good to know ya  
All I ever wanted was a chance to love ya  
Was a chance to give you the flame of my Heart  
But you were so sure we’re better apart  

Goodbye pretty baby, I did fucking try  
For more than two years I was in the sky  
I was on my knees from the very start  
But you were so sure we’re better apart  

Goodbye pretty baby, and again Hello  
I am really trying but can’t let it go  
I do love you so, with all of my Heart  
Please don’t be so sure we’re better apart
