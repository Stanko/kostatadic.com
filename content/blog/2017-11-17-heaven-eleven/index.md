When I finish my Thai language classes,  
I come to your store and I see your glasses,  
your beautiful smile and your shining eyes,  
as deep as the see, as bright as the skies…  

This is not an ad for 7–Eleven,  
but because of You, your store is like Heaven.  

_**15/11/2017**_
