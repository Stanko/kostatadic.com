**H**ope one sunny day you'll come really close  
**A**nd open your bud right under my nose  
**N**ow I can smell a beautiful rose  
**A**nd I want to taste the earth where it grows

**H**eartbroken and sad, I smile through my tears  
**A**lthough I know you don't feel the same  
**N**evertheless, the pain disappears  
**A**s I think of true meaning of your name
