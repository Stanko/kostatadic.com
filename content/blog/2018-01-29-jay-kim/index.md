**J**ust want you to know: I really think that  
**A** moment with you is worth living for.  
**Y**ou're the hottest woman I have ever met,

**K**issable and smart, elegant, and more...  
**I** would give my life to be close to Jay,  
**M**aking her lips smile when she's not okay.

_28/08/2017_
