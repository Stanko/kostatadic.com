– Oh I love you too, I feel just the same  
And I’d love to stand right next to your flame  
But the problem is, and ain’t that a shame  
You seem like the One, but have the wrong name  

– But you said you had never played that game  
That you’d never judged creatures by their name  
A name is a name, no one is to blame  
And it’s not important if you feel the same  

– Oh please don’t you start with your fairy tales  
This is the real world, full of zoos and jails  
You’re the hottest creature I have ever met  
But the dogs would hate me sleeping with a cat  

– Well, I think you’re right, this is a mistake  
Thought you were like me, but you’re just a fake  
And it’s not your name that’s bringing me pain  
You seem like the One, but have the wrong brain
