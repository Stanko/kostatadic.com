I fucking believed you, but you changed your skin  
You were a Tom Sawyer and you were a Finn  
And you were a Joe, a knife in your hand  
…but you were the Queen, and you were a Friend  

I fucking believed you, but you stole my skin  
You caught me and raped me from toe to the chin  
And I don’t regret it, and I still remember  
And that’s who I am: I keep it forever  

I fucking believed you, I gave you my Heart  
In your loving presence I was never smart  
I was never acting, nor trying to win  
And you were the You, and then changed your skin
